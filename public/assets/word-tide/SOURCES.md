## Parachute

`parachute.glb` is **Parachute** by **Poly by Google**, downloaded from
https://poly.pizza/m/3Z7vJ96JIEB on September 30, 2026.
License: CC BY 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Original self-contained GLB: https://static.poly.pizza/cd4e55aa-0c83-4bc4-b2ee-a0c4a9506e31.glb
The bundled file is unchanged. Runtime geometry keeps the modeled canopy and replaces
its fine suspension lines and handle with thicker rope meshes connected to the shoulders.
The canopy inflates, sways, and folds after landing. Credit appears during parachute descent.
